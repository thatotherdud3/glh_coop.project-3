OUR FARMERS

The GLH framework was designed by local growers for the local community to eliminate the compromises of the industrial food system. It is a system built on mutual respect rather than global convenience.


We don’t ask you to source locally out of a sense of duty, but out of a recognition of excellence. It is a refined approach to community building, one where the superior quality of the harvest is a natural byproduct of a society that looks after its own.
